These files are copies of the repository's ranges/ folder (the preflop range
library solved by K15 GTO's own preflop solver; see ranges/README.txt for how
they were made, their approximations and the file format). Flutter can only
bundle assets inside the app package, hence the copies. After regenerating
ranges/, copy the six *.txt files here again; test/solved_library_test.dart
fails while the copies differ.
